GymTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

GymTracker is a workout tracking app for Android phones, Wear OS watches and the web. It is designed to process only the data needed to provide its features. This policy explains what data GymTracker processes, why, where it is stored, and what rights you have. It covers the app published as com.otin.gym_tracker and the web app at gym-tracker-44a5f.web.app.

The short version. If you do not sign in, GymTracker does not upload the data you record to our servers. If you do sign in, your workouts are stored in your own private area of Google Cloud Firestore so they reach your other devices — but the heart-rate readings taken during them stay on your devices unless you separately switch that on. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising, profiling or analytics. You can delete everything from inside the app.

2. What data GymTracker processes

Data stored locally on your device

When you use GymTracker without signing in, your data is stored only in a database on the device itself. This may include:

  • workout routines and exercises
  • sets, repetitions, weights, durations and other workout measurements
  • workout history
  • heart-rate readings measured by the sensor on your watch during a workout

When you are not signed in, GymTracker does not upload your workout or heart-rate data to our servers. A phone and a paired Wear OS watch exchange routines and workouts directly with each other over the Wear Data Layer — a device-to-device link between two of your own devices that does not pass through us.

Data stored when you sign in

Signing in enables sync so the same data appears on your phone, watch and in the browser. Data is stored using Google Firebase, under a path keyed to your account, and is readable and writable only by that account.

3. Health and heart-rate data

Heart-rate information can constitute health data, which is a special category of personal data under the GDPR. We treat it as sensitive.

Heart-rate readings are measured by the sensor on your watch during a workout and stored on that device. GymTracker only processes heart-rate data when you use a device that records it.

Heart-rate readings are not stored in your account unless you explicitly ask for it. Signing in is not that request: it syncs your routines and workout history, and by itself leaves every reading on your devices. Storing heart rate in the cloud is a separate opt-in — Store heart rate in my account — which is off until you turn it on. It is offered as a checkbox when you sign in on the web, and as a switch on the Account screen everywhere; leaving it unchecked signs you in normally. While it is off, your workouts still sync; their readings do not.

When it is on, readings are uploaded with the workout they belong to, over an encrypted connection, into your private area, which no other user can read.

You may withdraw this consent at any time by switching it back off. Doing so also deletes the readings already stored in your account: each workout is rewritten without them. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place beforehand.

Heart rate is also exchanged directly between your own phone and watch over the Wear Data Layer so a workout run on the watch appears on the phone. That is a device-to-device link and is unaffected by this setting, because it does not pass through us.

Health Connect

On Android, GymTracker can publish finished workouts to Health Connect so they appear alongside data from your other health apps. This is off by default and you turn it on yourself on the Account screen.

  • The app requests write permission only: WRITE_EXERCISE and WRITE_HEART_RATE.
  • It writes the start and end time of a workout, its name, and the heart-rate readings recorded during it.
  • It never reads anything from Health Connect. No data of any kind is taken out of Health Connect by this app, and no data shown in GymTracker comes from Health Connect.
  • What it writes stays on your device, in Health Connect's own store. This is separate from, and additional to, the cloud sync described above.
  • You can switch it off on the Account screen at any time, and revoke the permission in Health Connect itself.

5. Local-only use

You can use GymTracker without creating an account. In local-only mode your workout data remains on your device and is not uploaded to GymTracker's cloud storage. Creating an account is optional and is only necessary if you want features such as synchronization between devices.

6. Cloud storage and Google Firebase

When you sign in, GymTracker uses Google Firebase to provide authentication and cloud storage. Google processes certain data on our behalf as a service provider and data processor. Your data is not provided to Google for Google's independent advertising or profiling purposes.

Depending on the Firebase services used, data may be processed on Google's global infrastructure, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on legally recognized transfer mechanisms and safeguards, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

For more information about Google's privacy practices and Firebase's data processing, please refer to Google's applicable privacy and Firebase documentation.

7. Who we share your data with

We do not:

  • sell your personal data
  • use your workout data for advertising
  • use your health data for advertising
  • provide your data to third parties for their independent purposes
  • use third-party analytics to profile your use of GymTracker

The app also contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics or usage tracking, no crash-reporting SDK, and it requests no location, contacts, photos or microphone access.

Google Firebase is used as infrastructure for authentication and cloud storage and may process data on our behalf. We may also disclose information where required by law or where necessary to protect the security and integrity of the service.

An AI assistant you connect

You can connect an AI assistant that you use yourself (for example Claude or ChatGPT) to your GymTracker account. Connecting is your decision: you sign in to GymTracker, are shown the name of the assistant and choose Allow or Deny. If you allow it, the assistant can read your routines, your workouts and your exercise library. It cannot read heart-rate data (even if you have turned on storing it in your account) and it cannot change anything.

The data is sent to the assistant only when you ask it something that needs your training data, and is then handled by the provider of that assistant under its own terms and privacy policy. GymTracker does not send your data to the assistant on its own.

To connect, GymTracker keeps a record of the connection: your account identifier, which assistant it is, and sign-in tokens that are stored only in hashed form. These records are deleted automatically when they expire, at the latest about 30 days after the assistant was last used. To disconnect, remove GymTracker in the assistant's own settings. The assistant stops renewing its access, which then ends within an hour, and a connection that is not used for 30 days expires by itself. Deleting your account as described in section 10 ends it as well.

8. Who can see your data

Access is restricted to your own account by server-side security rules: a request may only read or write data under its own account identifier. Other GymTracker users cannot access your data.

9. Data retention

  • If you use GymTracker without an account, your workout data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app and its data.
  • If you have an account, your account and associated workout data are retained for as long as your account remains active.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud data at any time.
  • Some limited technical or security information may be retained for longer where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
  • Where deletion from backups or other temporary systems cannot occur immediately, such data is isolated from normal use and deleted in accordance with the applicable retention cycle.

10. Deleting your account and data

  • Delete individual items — routines and workouts can be deleted in the app at any time. A small record that an item was deleted (its identifier and the time) is kept so the deletion also reaches your other devices; it contains no training data.
  • Delete your whole account — Account screen, "Delete account". This deletes your sign-in credentials and everything stored under your account on the server, and offers to erase the local copy on that device too. This cannot be undone.
  • You can also delete locally stored workout data directly from your device. Uninstalling the app removes the local database on that device, but does not by itself delete anything already synced to your account — use the delete option above for that.

When your account is deleted, we delete the personal data associated with it from our active systems, subject to information we are legally required or otherwise legitimately entitled to retain.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Cloud data is protected using the security mechanisms provided by Google Firebase, including authenticated access and database security rules, and is transmitted over encrypted connections.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. However, we aim to use safeguards appropriate to the type of data processed by GymTracker.
