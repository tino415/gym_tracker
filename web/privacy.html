GymTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026

GymTracker is a workout tracking app for Android phones, Wear OS watches and the web. This policy explains exactly what it stores, what leaves your device, and how to delete it. It covers the app published as com.otin.gym_tracker and the web app at gym-tracker-44a5f.web.app.

The short version. If you do not sign in, nothing you record ever leaves your devices. If you do sign in, your workouts — including heart-rate readings taken during them — are stored in your own private area of Google Cloud Firestore so they reach your other devices. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for analytics. You can delete everything from inside the app.

Signed out: local only

Without an account the app keeps all data in a database on the device itself. Nothing is uploaded to any server. A phone and a paired Wear OS watch exchange routines and workouts directly with each other over the Wear Data Layer — that is a device-to-device link between two of your own devices and does not pass through us.

Signed in: what is stored in the cloud

Signing in enables sync so the same data appears on your phone, watch and in the browser. Data is stored using Google Firebase, under a path keyed to your account, and is readable and writable only by that account.

Heart-rate readings are health data, and we treat them as sensitive. They are recorded by the sensor on your watch during a workout, stored on the device, and — when you are signed in — uploaded with that workout. They are transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored in your private area, which no other user can read.

Health Connect

On Android, GymTracker can publish finished workouts to Health Connect so they appear alongside data from your other health apps. This is off by default and you turn it on yourself on the Account screen.

  • The app requests write permission only: WRITE_EXERCISE and WRITE_HEART_RATE.
  • It writes the start and end time of a workout, its name, and the heart-rate readings recorded during it.
  • It never reads anything from Health Connect. No data of any kind is taken out of Health Connect by this app.
  • What it writes stays on your device, in Health Connect's own store. This is separate from, and additional to, the cloud sync described above.
  • You can switch it off on the Account screen at any time, and revoke the permission in Health Connect itself.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or usage tracking, and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • No selling of data, and no sharing with third parties for their own purposes.
  • No location data, no contacts, no photos, no microphone.
  • No reading of your data from Health Connect or any other health service.

The only third party involved is Google, which provides the authentication and database infrastructure the app runs on, and processes the data solely to provide that service.

Who can see your data

Access is restricted to your own account by server-side security rules: a request may only read or write data under its own account identifier. Nobody else using the app can reach your data.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Delete individual items — routines and workouts can be deleted in the app at any time. A small record that an item was deleted (its identifier and the time) is kept so the deletion also reaches your other devices; it contains no training data.
  • Delete your whole account — Account screen, "Delete account". This deletes your sign-in credentials and everything stored under your account on the server, and offers to erase the local copy on that device too. This cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the local database on that device, but does not by itself delete anything already synced to your account — use the delete option above for that.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. Access and correction are available directly in the app, and erasure through the account deletion described above. For anything else, get in touch using the address below.

Children

GymTracker is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected or how it is used, the date at the top of this page is updated and the change is described in the app's release notes.
